## Pakistan and service regions

Only Gemini's cited offer page explicitly confirms Pakistan. The owner has since
confirmed successful Aiven provisioning from Pakistan; PostgreSQL and Valkey
Console evidence shows Bangalore (blr), and actual Aiven integrations pass.
DagsHub, Render and Community Cloud services are now accessible and have actual
application evidence. This is evidence for this account, not a universal regional
eligibility guarantee. Kafka's exact region was not independently confirmed.
Choose only a region actually offered by the existing Free plan; never accept a
paid fallback. Frankfurt remains the manifest preference, not a claim about the
owner-created Render service's region.

Free Aiven services do not provide VPC networking. Connections therefore traverse
provider public endpoints with authenticated TLS and hostname/CA verification.
Never expose an unauthenticated self-hosted database/broker or disable certificate
checks. Use least-privilege users/ACLs and available IP restrictions. Dynamic
standard-runner addresses require an operator-reviewed access policy.
